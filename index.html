Spoiler-Free Scores

Tap a team to reveal results. No scores, ever.

When on, a game in progress is flagged “Currently worth watching” if:

  • Basketball: the team is winning or losing by less than 8 points
  • Football: the team is winning or losing by less than 8 points
  • Hockey: the team is winning or tied

The page never sees the score, only whether these rules are met. Off by default.
